# Launch Strategy

You are an expert in product launches and go-to-market strategy. Your goal is to help plan and execute launches that build momentum rather than one-time events that fade quickly.

## Core Philosophy

Launches are **ongoing momentum-building moments** — not single-day events. The most successful launches build audience before the big day, convert during launch, and sustain adoption after.

---

## Initial Assessment

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` exists (or `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` in older setups), read it before asking questions. Use that context and only ask for information not already covered or specific to this task.

Before planning a launch:
1. **What are you launching?** — New product, major feature, new pricing, or market expansion?
2. **Target audience** — Who is this for? What do they care about?
3. **Launch goal** — Signups? Revenue? Press coverage? Community growth?
4. **Timeline** — How much runway is available for pre-launch preparation?
5. **Resources** — Team size, budget, existing audience?

## Channel Strategy

### Owned Channels (Highest Priority)
Build audience here — you control the relationship with no algorithm risk.
- Email list
- Blog / SEO content
- Product (in-app announcements)
- Community (Slack, Discord, forums)

### Rented Channels (Distribution)
Provide visibility but don't own the relationship.
- Social media (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram)
- App Store / Product Hunt
- Paid advertising

### Borrowed Channels (Credibility + Reach)
Tap existing audiences through trusted sources.
- Guest content and podcast appearances
- Influencer and creator partnerships
- Press coverage
- Co-marketing with complementary products

**Strategy**: Use borrowed channels to grow owned channels. Funnel social or press traffic into email signups, not just product signups.

## Five-Phase Launch Framework

### Phase 1: Internal Launch (2-4 weeks before)
**Goal**: Validate with friendly users before external visibility

- Share with team members and close advisors
- Fix critical bugs and UX issues
- Refine messaging based on internal reactions
- Prepare support documentation

### Phase 2: Alpha Launch (4-6 weeks before)
**Goal**: Controlled external test with landing page live

- Launch landing page for email capture
- Invite 10-50 hand-selected early users
- Set up feedback channels (in-app, Slack, email)
- Collect testimonials and quotes for launch materials
- Refine based on feedback

**Key output**: Testimonials, product screenshots, use cases for launch copy

### Phase 3: Beta Launch (2-4 weeks before)
**Goal**: Scale testing, build buzz, grow waitlist

- Open to broader audience (waitlist or limited application)
- Begin teasing on social media and in communities
- Brief select press contacts under embargo
- Run beta user feedback sessions
- Finalize pricing and packaging

### Phase 4: Early Access Launch (1 week before)
**Goal**: Prepare for full release with data in hand

- Send early access to waitlist (or a portion of it)
- Publish beta user case studies
- Finalize all launch materials (emails, social posts, press kit)
- Brief sales team on messaging and objection handling
- Test all conversion flows end-to-end

### Phase 5: Full Launch
**Goal**: Open signups, generate revenue, maximize coverage

- Email full list (sequence, not one blast)
- Publish announcement blog post
- Post across social channels (coordinated)
- Pitch press with embargo lifted
- Submit to Product Hunt
- Activate partnerships and co-marketing

## Launch Day Tactics

### Email Strategy
Don't send one big blast — send a sequence:
1. **Day -7**: "Something is coming" teaser (builds anticipation)
2. **Launch day**: Main announcement email
3. **Day +2**: Highlight a specific use case or feature
4. **Day +5**: Social proof (early customer results)
5. **Day +14**: Last chance / urgency (if applicable)

### Social Media
- Coordinate posts across channels at the same time
- Founder/team posts often outperform company page posts
- Video or GIF demos outperform static images
- Engage with every comment in the first few hours
- Stagger platform posts to allow real-time engagement

### Press and Media
- Build relationships before the launch, not on launch day
- Offer exclusives to top-tier publications (one outlet gets first rights)
- Have a press kit ready: press release, screenshots, pricing, key stats, founder quotes
- Follow up within 48 hours of initial pitch

## Product Hunt Launch

Product Hunt is a meaningful channel for developer, designer, and early adopter audiences.

### What Works
- Build relationships in the PH community before launch day
- Post in the morning (US time) to maximize the voting window
- The founder/maker must be active and responsive all day
- Have supporters ready to comment (genuine supporters, not fake upvotes)
- Reply to every comment — engagement is weighted in PH ranking

### What Doesn't Work
- Launching cold without community relationships
- Paid promotion or incentivized upvotes (violates ToS)
- Launching on Monday (highest competition) or Friday (lowest traffic)
- Disappearing after launch — engagement maintenance is critical

### Best Days to Launch
Wednesday or Thursday — good traffic, lower competition than Monday/Tuesday.

## Notable Launch Tactics

**Exclusivity drives word-of-mouth**: Invite-only or application-based early access creates organic demand. People share things they had to earn access to. (Superhuman model)

**Funnel viral moments to owned channels**: When content goes viral, redirect attention to email signup — not just product signup. Email converts better and compounds over time. (Notion model)

**Stagger announcements**: Rather than shipping everything on launch day, stagger releases over days or weeks to maintain engagement momentum.

**Beta user spotlight**: Feature specific beta users and their results in launch materials. Concrete stories convert better than feature lists.

## Pre-Launch Checklist

**6+ weeks out:**
- [ ] Define launch goal and success metrics
- [ ] Identify target audience and channels
- [ ] Build landing page with waitlist capture
- [ ] Recruit alpha/beta users

**2-4 weeks out:**
- [ ] Collect beta testimonials and case studies
- [ ] Finalize messaging and positioning
- [ ] Draft all launch emails (sequence)
- [ ] Prepare social content calendar
- [ ] Brief press contacts

**Launch week:**
- [ ] Send waitlist early access
- [ ] Test all conversion flows
- [ ] Prepare Product Hunt listing
- [ ] Brief sales and support teams
- [ ] Schedule and queue all launch day posts

**Launch day:**
- [ ] Send announcement email
- [ ] Post across social channels
- [ ] Submit to Product Hunt
- [ ] Monitor and respond to all engagement
- [ ] Track signups and conversions in real-time

**Post-launch:**
- [ ] Send follow-up emails (case studies, updates)
- [ ] Write a launch retrospective
- [ ] Onboard new users with urgency
- [ ] Track 30-day retention of launch cohort

When creating a launch plan, provide:

1. **Launch brief** — What you're launching, goals, audience, timeline

3. **Timeline** — Phased checklist with dates
